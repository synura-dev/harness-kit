---
name: harness
description: Dung cong CLI cua harness-kit de doc du lieu/audit va chay doctor. Dung khi can xem audit, kiem tra cau hinh guard, hoac tra lenh harness.
---

Chay qua cong duy nhat: `harness [<domain> [lenh]]` (bin/ cua plugin, co trong PATH cua Bash tool; du phong: `node "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/bin/harness" ...`).
Du lieu cua du an nam o `<du an>/.harness/data` (tu gitignore). PLUGIN_ROOT chi doc.
Kiem tra cai dat: `harness doctor`.
